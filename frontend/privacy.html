Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Multiplayer Chess is an online chess platform operated by Aditya Gupta. This policy describes information handled when you play games, create an account, sign in with Google or participate in tournaments.

Information we handle

  • Account information: your username, account identifier, email address and rating. New password accounts require email verification. Password accounts store a password hash, not a readable password. Google sign-in also supplies a verified email address. Older password accounts can add a recovery email after proving account ownership.
  • Google sign-in: Google and Supabase process your sign-in. The requested permissions are basic identity scopes: openid, email and profile. Supabase may receive your name and profile picture; the chess backend uses your verified email and stable Supabase identity identifier to create or recover your chess account. Your initial chess username may be derived from the part of your email before the @ symbol.
  • Gameplay: player identifiers, moves, move timings, results, ratings, game history, tournament registrations, check-ins, pairings, standings and result corrections.
  • Safety and diagnostics: connection information such as IP addresses, request and error logs, and gameplay-based anti-cheat analysis or reports.

We do not receive your Google password. Google sign-in does not request access to your Gmail messages, Drive files or contacts.

Verification and password-recovery requests use expiring, single-use email links. The database stores token digests rather than usable link tokens. Verification links expire after 30 minutes and password links after 15 minutes; expired challenge records are cleaned up during later email requests. The email sender processes the recipient address and link to deliver the message through Gmail SMTP.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate you, maintain sessions, match players, operate games and tournaments, save results and replays, calculate ratings, investigate unfair play, and diagnose service failures. Google identity information is used for account authentication and account management, not advertising.

What other players can see

Your chess username, rating, game participation, moves, results and tournament standings may be visible in leaderboards, tournament pages, live spectating and replays. Do not choose a username that contains information you want to keep private. Your email address, password hash and session tokens are not intended to be public player information.

Service providers and sharing

The frontend is hosted on Vercel, the backend on Google Cloud Run, and the database and Google sign-in service on Supabase. Google also processes the Google sign-in itself. These providers handle information needed to deliver and secure their services under their own policies. Hosting and database services may process information outside your country.

We do not sell Google user data or use it for targeted advertising. Access may be needed by the operator to support the service, investigate abuse or respond to lawful requests.

Provider policies: Google, Supabase, and Vercel.

Browser storage and sessions

The app uses browser local storage for its refresh token, preferences and local progress such as puzzle streaks and onboarding state. It also uses temporary browser storage and memory for navigation and active sessions. Signing out revokes the current application session. Clearing site storage removes local settings and sign-in recovery information, but does not delete server-side account or game records. Google and our hosting providers may use their own cookies or storage.

Retention and your choices

Account and gameplay records remain in the service database unless removed by the operator; there is no automatic account-deletion schedule in the current release. Operational logs and provider backups have separate retention settings. Removing browser storage or revoking Google access does not by itself delete your chess account or saved games.

To request access, correction or deletion of your information, email aditya.gupta.cloud.1@gmail.com. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. We will explain any records that need to remain for security, shared game or tournament integrity, or legal obligations. There is no self-service account-deletion button in this release.

You can stop using Google sign-in or revoke its access through your Google Account's third-party connections settings.

Security

The service uses HTTPS/WSS in production, password hashing, session revocation and restricted database access. Authentication payloads also use a sealed-envelope layer. These controls reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Never send passwords, private keys or session tokens in a support email.

Policy updates and contact

Changes will be published here with an updated date. For privacy questions, contact aditya.gupta.cloud.1@gmail.com.
